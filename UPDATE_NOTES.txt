V18 update
- Supabase setup now creates/upgrades the products and product_images tables and sets public read/admin-only write access.
- Product image storage policies are made admin-only for uploads, updates, and removals.
- Admin product loading now shows a clear database error instead of silently displaying zero.
- Editing a product preserves its current image gallery unless replacement photos or the remove option are selected; replaced/deleted product image files are cleaned up when possible.
- Delivered and cancelled orders can be archived from the active admin order list and restored later. Archiving preserves the order row and customer order history.
- Supabase setup adds the archived_at column safely to existing order tables without deleting order data.
